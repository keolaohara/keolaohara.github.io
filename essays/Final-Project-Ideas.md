#Final Project
##Idea 1: Food Fitness Feed
Problem: Most college students are in a new area when living near campus. Not everyone has accessibility to transportation every time they want to purchase food that aligns with their goals such as weight loss or saving money. Students may not know of places to find that are near campus.

Solution: Our solution to this problem is a website that helps students find food options anywhere on Oahu. Students will be able to enter their budget, nutritional goals, and dietary preferences and restrictions and transportation options to recommend restaurants, groceries stores, and campus options to meet their needs. Students will be able to find affordable and nutritious meals from the website without having to do research on unfamiliar places. On top of this we will have a community dashboard where students with similar needs can share their opinions of the restaurants for others before deciding. The main features of the app will be to compare meal prices, find nutritional information, and travel distance to suggest better options for students.

##Approach
This website will be an interactive island wide information hub, where users can search for healthy food alternatives to help with their fitness goals. The website will allow people to enter  different inputs (cost, vegan, ingredients). To use Food Fitness Feed, the user will input their desired taste, and be able to look over posts that are most relevant to their search. Each post includes calories, rating, store and cost. 

The user will be limited to some features unless they sign up using their @hawaii.edu account or email. An existing user will be able to see the specific ingredients of their item or meal, see other users reviews of that post, rate and give feedback on posts, and be able to post their own independent recommendations for others. They will also access other minor features like being able to favorite posts and other samples. 

To delegate the website there will be admin access, that can flag inappropriate posts, remove/ban users, and monitor information to protect personal information.

##Use Case Ideas
- Not logged-in user looks for vendors with their inputted budget
- Logged-in user can rate and write reviews for vendors
- Logged-in user can set dietary preferences and nutritional goals and look for vendors

##Beyond the Basics
- Map for vendors
- Vendor meal costs
- Vendor operating hours 
- Allow students to write reviews and ratings for vendors (user must be logged in)
- Vendor meal nutrition information
