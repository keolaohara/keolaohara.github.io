For this project, I designed and assembled small camera-equipped drones using pre-made components. The goal was to create a lightweight and functional drone that could be controlled reliably while capturing video from the air. We considered factors such as the placement of components, weight distribution, stability, and camera positioning when developing the overall design.

The drones were assembled using pre-made parts, including motors, propellers, flight controller, battery, and camera system. I helped determine how the components would be arranged and assisted with assembling and connecting the different parts. After assembly, we tested the drones to make sure they could take off, remain stable during flight, respond properly to controls, and capture usable camera footage. When problems occurred, we inspected the components and made adjustments to improve the drone's performance.

Through this project, I learned more about how mechanical design, electronics, and hardware integration come together in a complete system. Working with the drones gave me hands-on experience assembling components, troubleshooting hardware, and understanding how design decisions such as weight and component placement can affect performance. I also gained experience working collaboratively and learned the importance of testing and making adjustments when turning a design into a functional product.
